# Highto

Highto is a personal wellbeing, safety, family and smart-living platform with one AI companion: JORDAN.

## Product areas
1. Healthcare
2. Safety & Emergency
3. Smart Living
4. Family & Protection
5. Personal & Account

The first frontend starter is being added to this repository. Live AI, authentication, health-provider connections, wearable sync, smart-home control, family sharing and emergency-service integrations must be implemented and configured separately. Do not present simulated integrations as live services.
